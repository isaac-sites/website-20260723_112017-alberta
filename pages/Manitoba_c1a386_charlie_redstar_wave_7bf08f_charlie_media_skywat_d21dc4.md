## Introduction

The [Charlie Redstar wave]({{ 'charlie-redstar/' | relative_url }}) became a [Manitoba]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-manitobas-ufo/' | relative_url }}) phenomenon not simply because unusual lights were reported, but because people began gathering night after night to watch for them. Around Carman in 1975, roadside skywatching evolved from a handful of curious observers into a regular social event. Local newspapers, radio and television amplified the story, encouraging more visitors and creating a feedback loop in which expectations spread almost as quickly as the reports themselves. This media-driven atmosphere is an important part of understanding the Charlie Redstar episode. It helps explain both why the wave became so widely remembered and why later investigators have treated some testimony with caution, even while accepting that many people genuinely believed they had witnessed something unusual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canadianuforeport.com/survey/essay/MBUFOSurvey.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canadianuforeport.com">[canadianuforeport.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canadianuforeport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The M-Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 2025</span></span></span>

## The roadside viewing crowds around Carman

One of the most distinctive features of the Charlie Redstar episode was that it transformed UFO watching from isolated observation into a public gathering.

Following the first reports near Bob and Elaine Diemert's property outside Carman, increasing numbers of residents began driving to rural roads overlooking the southern horizon. By early May 1975, people were arriving nightly in family cars and pickup trucks, waiting together for darkness in the hope of seeing the now-famous red light. Unlike many UFO reports, which rely on a single witness or small group, Charlie Redstar often had dozens of observers present at roughly the same time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canadianuforeport.com/survey/essay/MBUFOSurvey.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canadianuforeport.com">[canadianuforeport.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canadianuforeport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The M-Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 2025</span></span></span>

The gatherings soon took on the character of a local attraction. Witnesses recalled vehicles lining gravel roads, people sharing binoculars and cameras, and newcomers asking experienced observers where to look. Residents from Winnipeg and elsewhere in southern Manitoba travelled to Carman specifically to participate. According to later historical accounts based on contemporary reporting, traffic became heavy enough that informal "chases" occasionally developed when observers believed the light had shifted position, with cars racing between viewing locations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canadianuforeport.com/survey/essay/MBUFOSurvey.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canadianuforeport.com">[canadianuforeport.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canadianuforeport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The M-Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 2025</span></span></span>

This social setting mattered because witnesses were no longer observing independently. They were reacting to one another's excitement, discussing what they thought they had seen and often interpreting ambiguous lights collectively.

## How newspapers spread sightings and expectations

Local newspapers did more than report the story; they helped turn Charlie Redstar into a recognisable public character.

Coverage rapidly expanded beyond straightforward sighting reports. The recurring light acquired the nickname "Charlie Redstar", giving scattered observations a memorable identity that readers could easily follow from week to week. Instead of many unrelated lights, the press increasingly presented a continuing story about a familiar visitor returning to southern Manitoba.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://utpdistribution.com/9781459737808/charlie-red-star/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: utpdistribution.com">[University of Toronto Press Distribution]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">utpdistribution.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">University of Toronto Press Distribution Charlie Red Star</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">University of Toronto Press DistributionCharlie Red StarAugust 12, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 12, 2017</span></span></span>

The media attention extended well beyond hard news.


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Local papers covered fresh sightings and interviews with witnesses.
* Businesses adopted Charlie Redstar in advertisements and promotions, joking that customers should "Shop where Charlie Redstar shops!"
* Provincial and national reporters visited Carman as curiosity grew.
* Sensational publications, including the *National Enquirer*, also arrived to interview witnesses, exposing the story to a much wider audience.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canadianuforeport.com/survey/essay/MBUFOSurvey.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canadianuforeport.com">[canadianuforeport.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canadianuforeport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The M-Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 2025</span></span></span>

</div>

This publicity changed how people approached the phenomenon. Instead of unexpectedly encountering an unexplained light, many visitors travelled to Carman expecting to see Charlie. That expectation naturally influenced where they looked, how long they watched and how they interpreted distant lights on the horizon.

Media coverage also encouraged more people to report unusual experiences. Individuals who might previously have dismissed an odd light as unimportant now had a recognised framework into which they could fit their own observations.

## When public excitement began to contaminate testimony

The popularity of the skywatches creates one of the central challenges in evaluating the Charlie Redstar wave today.

Modern investigators generally distinguish between sincere testimony and independent evidence. There is little reason to doubt that many observers honestly reported what they believed they saw. The difficulty is that testimony collected in a highly social environment is vulnerable to several well-understood influences.

As crowds gathered repeatedly:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* observers pointed out lights to one another before everyone had formed an independent impression;
* conversations during and after sightings encouraged a shared interpretation;
* newspaper descriptions supplied recurring details that later witnesses sometimes repeated;
* the expectation of seeing "Charlie" made ordinary celestial or aircraft lights more likely to be viewed as part of the ongoing mystery.

</div>

These effects do not require dishonesty. They are familiar features of eyewitness psychology and have been documented in many historical waves of unusual aerial reports. They make it harder to determine which details originated with direct observation and which developed through discussion and repeated retelling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2026/04/credible-people-incredible-things/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skeptical Inquirer Credible People, Incredible Things &#124; Skeptical Inquirer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skeptical Inquirer Credible People, Incredible Things &#124; Skeptical Inquirer</span></span></span>

This is especially important because descriptions of Charlie Redstar varied considerably. While many accounts mention a pulsating red or orange light, reports also included discs, structured craft, beams, changing shapes and dramatic manoeuvres. The growing public narrative may have encouraged witnesses to interpret different stimuli as manifestations of the same phenomenon rather than separate events.

## Why the media wave matters to Manitoba's UFO history

Charlie Redstar remains significant partly because it illustrates how a regional UFO wave can become a cultural event as well as an investigative one.

The nightly gatherings created an unusually large community of observers, making the episode far more visible than many isolated Canadian sightings. Newspapers preserved the story, attracted additional witnesses and ensured that Charlie Redstar entered Manitoba folklore rather than disappearing after a few isolated reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://firesidecanada.ca/charlie-redstar-manitobas-friendly-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: firesidecanada.ca">[firesidecanada.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">firesidecanada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fireside Canada: A Podcast About Canadian Legends, Lies &amp; Lore</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fireside Canada: A Podcast About Canadian Legends, Lies &amp; Lore</span></span></span>

At the same time, that success complicates historical assessment. The same publicity that preserved valuable witness accounts also increased the possibility of expectation, selective attention and social reinforcement. Later researchers therefore tend to treat the wave as both a genuine period of widespread reporting and a textbook example of how media attention can amplify an unfolding mystery without necessarily proving that every reported sighting involved the same unexplained object. This balanced interpretation helps explain why Charlie Redstar remains one of Manitoba's best-known UFO episodes while continuing to generate debate over exactly what people were seeing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canadianuforeport.com/survey/essay/MBUFOSurvey.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canadianuforeport.com">[canadianuforeport.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canadianuforeport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The M-Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 2025</span></span></span>

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
