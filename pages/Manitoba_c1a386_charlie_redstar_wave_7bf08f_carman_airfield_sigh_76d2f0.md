## Introduction

The sighting reported by Bob and Elaine Diemert near their private airfield outside Carman on 10 April 1975 is widely treated as the beginning of the [Charlie Redstar wave]({{ 'charlie-redstar/' | relative_url }}) in southern Manitoba. It matters because it gave the later flap its best-known origin story and inspired months of public observation around the Diemert property. At the same time, it is also an example of how an influential UFO case can rest on surprisingly limited evidence. Contemporary [official records]({{ 'official-records/' | relative_url }}) confirm that a report was made, but they do not independently establish many of the details that later became central to the story, including the object's precise size, altitude, speed or shape. Understanding what is documented, and what was added through later retellings, is essential for judging the Carman incident within Manitoba's UFO history.

## What Really Happened Over the Carman Airfield?

### The Diemerts' reported encounter on 10 April 1975

According to the best-known account, aircraft builder and pilot Robert ("Bob") Diemert and his wife Elaine were walking towards their private airfield near Carman during the evening of 10 April 1975 when they noticed a bright red light approaching from the west. They later described it as pulsating and, in subsequent interviews, as appearing to surround or reveal a domed, disc-like object. The light reportedly hovered for several minutes before changing direction and departing at low altitude above nearby trees.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[mbnews101.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fifty years have passed since charlie red star phenomenon in 1975</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101March 20, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

The encounter quickly became more than a private sighting. Friends, neighbours and eventually large crowds began visiting roads around the Diemert property, particularly from early May onwards, hoping to witness the same recurring light. Those gatherings transformed a single report into the public phenomenon that became known as "Charlie Redstar".<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[mbnews101.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fifty years have passed since charlie red star phenomenon in 1975</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101March 20, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

Even at this early stage, however, there is an important distinction between the initial event and the later folklore. The April encounter involved two principal witnesses. The widespread reputation of Charlie Redstar developed afterwards, through repeated observations by many different people under varying conditions rather than through one decisive, well-documented incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[mbnews101.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fifty years have passed since charlie red star phenomenon in 1975</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101March 20, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

## What the RCMP record confirms and leaves uncertain

The strongest contemporary evidence is not a photograph or physical trace but the existence of official reporting. The Royal Canadian Mounted Police recorded Diemert's report and forwarded information through the National Research Council's UFO reporting system. Canada's archived UFO database contains numerous records associated with the Carman sightings beginning with the 10 April 1975 incident, demonstrating that authorities documented the reports rather than dismissing them outright.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/databases/ufo/001057-110.01-e.php?PHPSESSID=f54dgejii9oknikiu11cnkej67ucni8e10jc372hovs78ub3ubq0&amp;interval=20&amp;q1=&amp;q2=&amp;q3=Carman&amp;q4=&amp;q8=" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: collectionscanada.gc.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">collectionscanada.gc.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada ARCHIVED</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaARCHIVED - Results - Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The Search for the Unknown - Library and Archives Canada...</span></span></span>

What the official documentation does **not** do is independently verify many of the characteristics repeated in later accounts. Depending on the source consulted, the object has been described as approximately 50 feet long, flying about 300 feet above the ground, travelling around 50 km/h, or displaying a clearly visible dome. Those measurements are generally traceable to witness descriptions reproduced in later publications rather than to independently measured observations. The available records do not contain radar tracking, calibrated photography, instrument readings or multiple synchronised observations capable of establishing the object's true dimensions or motion with confidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cassiopaea.org/forum/threads/charlie-red-star.54208/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cassiopaea.org">[Cassiopaea]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cassiopaea.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Charlie Red Star &#124; Cassiopaea Forum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Charlie Red Star &#124; Cassiopaea Forum...</span></span></span>

This distinction is important because estimating the size or speed of a distant light without a known range is inherently unreliable. A bright object whose distance cannot be measured may appear dramatically larger or slower than it actually is.

## Why the evidence cannot establish size, height or speed

The Carman case illustrates a common problem in UFO investigations: eyewitnesses can confidently describe what they perceived while still lacking the information needed to calculate an object's physical characteristics.

Several key pieces of information are missing:

* **No known range.** Without knowing how far away the light was, neither its physical size nor its altitude can be calculated.
* **No calibrated imagery.** No contemporary photographs or films from the initial 10 April encounter provide measurable reference points.
* **No corroborating instrument data.** Publicly available records do not include radar, flight telemetry or other independent measurements tied specifically to the first sighting.
* **Changing descriptions.** Different retellings vary in their estimates of shape, dimensions and movement, making it difficult to determine which details originated in the first report and which developed later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/databases/ufo/001057-110.01-e.php?PHPSESSID=f54dgejii9oknikiu11cnkej67ucni8e10jc372hovs78ub3ubq0&amp;interval=20&amp;q1=&amp;q2=&amp;q3=Carman&amp;q4=&amp;q8=" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: collectionscanada.gc.ca">[gc.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">collectionscanada.gc.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada ARCHIVED</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaARCHIVED - Results - Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The Search for the Unknown - Library and Archives Canada...</span></span></span>

Consequently, statements that Charlie Redstar was definitely a certain size, flew at a particular altitude or accelerated in a specific way go beyond what the surviving evidence can support. The witnesses reported what they believed they saw, but the available documentation cannot convert those impressions into verified measurements.

## How later retellings changed the original evidence

As Charlie Redstar became one of Canada's best-known UFO stories, the narrative gradually acquired more vivid detail.

Later books, documentaries, podcasts and online articles commonly describe a structured craft with a dome, spectacular manoeuvres, repeated close approaches and highly precise estimates of speed or altitude. Some accounts merge the 10 April encounter with observations made weeks or months later, creating the impression that all reported characteristics belonged to one continuous event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://believingthebizarre.com/charlie-red-star/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: believingthebizarre.com">[Believing the Bizarre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">believingthebizarre.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Believing the Bizarre Charlie Red Star: The Canadian UFO &#124; Ep. 84</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Believing the BizarreCharlie Red Star: The Canadian UFO &#124; Ep. 84 - Believing the Bizarre...</span></span></span>

This process is understandable. Repeated retelling often combines memories from different nights, media interviews and community recollections into a smoother narrative. The resulting story is more dramatic and easier to follow than the fragmented historical record.

For historians, however, separating the layers matters. The original event consisted of one reported encounter supported by official reporting paperwork. The richer descriptions emerged over time as the Charlie Redstar phenomenon expanded into a regional attraction followed by journalists, investigators and spectators. That does not mean later memories are false, but it does mean they should not automatically be treated as evidence for what happened on 10 April itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[mbnews101.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fifty years have passed since charlie red star phenomenon in 1975</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101March 20, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why the Carman sighting still matters

The Carman airfield sighting remains significant not because it conclusively demonstrated an unknown craft, but because it triggered one of [Manitoba]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-manitobas-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s most famous UFO waves while highlighting the limits of eyewitness evidence.

The official record confirms that experienced local witnesses reported an unusual red light and that Canadian authorities documented the case. It also shows that the surviving evidence leaves fundamental questions unresolved. The object's apparent size, altitude, speed and exact appearance cannot be established from the available records alone.

For that reason, the Carman sighting is best understood as the documented starting point of the Charlie Redstar wave rather than as a proven close encounter with a measurable unidentified craft. Its historical importance lies in how a modest amount of contemporaneous evidence evolved into one of the defining stories in Manitoba's UFO history, while the central observational gaps remained largely unchanged.
