## Introduction

Fredericton's importance in UFO history lies less in local sightings than in the archive assembled there by physicist and UFO researcher Stanton Friedman. Preserved at the Provincial Archives of [New Brunswick]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-new-brunswicks/' | relative_url }}), the collection offers something more valuable than a catalogue of unusual claims: it provides decades of correspondence, interview notes, recordings and research files that allow historians to compare what witnesses said at different points in time. Rather than asking whether a UFO event was "real", the archive makes it possible to examine how stories developed, what details remained stable, what changed, and how investigators themselves may have influenced later accounts. That makes the collection an important resource for understanding witness testimony as historical evidence rather than simply as proof for or against extraordinary claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation – Active History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation – Active HistoryMay 12, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

## Comparing first accounts with later versions

One of the strongest ways to assess witness reliability is to compare the earliest available statement with accounts recorded years or even decades later. Friedman's papers are especially valuable because he often retained both incoming correspondence and later interview material relating to the same individuals and cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation – Active History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation – Active HistoryMay 12, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

For historians, the first written account usually carries particular importance because it is closer in time to the reported event and less likely to have been influenced by later books, documentaries or public debate. Later interviews, however, can reveal how memories evolved. Researchers can ask questions such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* Did the central sequence of events remain consistent?
* Were new details added only after a case became famous?
* Did technical descriptions become more sophisticated over time?
* Were contradictions explained, ignored or overlooked?

</div>

These questions do not automatically establish whether a sighting occurred. Instead, they help distinguish between relatively stable recollections and narratives that expanded as witnesses revisited their experiences.

The Roswell controversy illustrates why this approach matters. Friedman's interviews with former military personnel helped revive the case from the late 1970s onwards. As additional witnesses emerged over subsequent decades, some accounts became more detailed while others conflicted with earlier testimony. Because Friedman frequently preserved correspondence alongside interview records, researchers can compare what individuals initially reported with what they later described after years of media attention.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/home/record?IdNumber=359660&amp;app=filvidandsou" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca">[Collection Search]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gc.ca.</span></span></span>

## How interview methods can shape testimony

Witness stories do not develop in isolation. The way investigators ask questions can influence the information eventually recorded.

Friedman's archive offers an opportunity to study the investigative process itself because it contains more than polished publications. It includes letters, drafts, notes, conference material and exchanges with supporters and critics. These documents can reveal whether particular ideas appeared first in a witness's own account or emerged during extended discussions with investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation – Active History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation – Active HistoryMay 12, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

Several mechanisms are worth considering when comparing interviews:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Repeated interviewing.** People often remember additional details after discussing an event many times. Some additions may reflect genuine recollection, while others may result from reconstructing memories.
* **Leading questions.** Questions that assume a particular explanation can unintentionally encourage witnesses to frame their memories in similar terms.
* **Exposure to publicity.** Books, television programmes and newspaper coverage can introduce details that later become incorporated into personal recollections, even when witnesses sincerely believe they always remembered them.
* **Community discussion.** Witnesses who communicate with other participants or researchers sometimes begin using common descriptions or interpretations.

</div>

These effects are well recognised within memory research generally and need not imply dishonesty. People may genuinely believe later versions of events even when those versions differ from their earliest statements.

For this reason, historians increasingly value archives that preserve the evolution of an investigation rather than only its final published conclusions. Friedman's papers provide precisely this kind of documentary trail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation – Active History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation – Active HistoryMay 12, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

## What contradictions can and cannot prove

Differences between early and late testimony deserve careful attention, but they should not be treated as automatic evidence either for fraud or for authenticity.

Contradictions may arise for several reasons:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* normal changes in human memory over long periods;
* misunderstandings during interviews;
* editing for publication;
* genuine corrections after new documents emerge;
* deliberate embellishment by some witnesses.

</div>

The archive therefore supports careful comparison rather than simple verdicts. If an early letter omits dramatic claims that appear only decades later, researchers may reasonably question why those details were absent originally. Equally, the absence of certain information in an early account does not necessarily prove that a witness fabricated later memories, especially if the interviewer initially focused on different questions.

Likewise, strong consistency across independent documents does not prove that an extraordinary event occurred. It demonstrates that testimony remained comparatively stable, which is only one element in assessing historical evidence. Physical evidence, official records, radar data, photographs and contemporary documentation still require separate evaluation.

## Why the Fredericton archive matters for evidence

The significance of the Stanton Friedman fonds extends beyond individual UFO cases. It allows researchers to study how controversial historical narratives are constructed over decades.

Because Friedman corresponded with witnesses, investigators, journalists, sceptics and government agencies, the archive preserves multiple perspectives surrounding the same controversies. Instead of relying solely on published books, researchers can examine drafts, private letters and earlier versions of stories to understand how interpretations developed over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation – Active History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation – Active HistoryMay 12, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

This is particularly valuable in Canadian UFO research because few collections preserve such a long-running documentary record assembled by a single investigator. Although many of the cases concern the United States, the archive's location in Fredericton gives New Brunswick an important place in preserving material used to evaluate some of North America's best-known UFO claims.

The collection also reminds researchers that archival work is a gradual process. The Provincial Archives of New Brunswick has described the Friedman fonds as exceptionally large and complex, containing extensive correspondence, research files and records that require careful arrangement and privacy review before broad public access can be provided.     var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
