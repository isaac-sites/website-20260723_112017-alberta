## Introduction

Bright fireballs are among the most dramatic sights in [British Columbia]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-british/' | relative_url }})'s night sky, and they regularly generate reports from people convinced they have just seen an aircraft crash into a nearby mountain, forest or lake. In reality, the opposite is usually true. A large meteor burns up tens of kilometres above the Earth, yet the human brain interprets it as a much lower, nearer object because the night sky provides very few reliable depth cues. Across the Victoria region, the Fraser Valley and British Columbia's Interior, the same fireball has often prompted simultaneous reports from communities separated by hundreds of kilometres, demonstrating that [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses/' | relative_url }}) were observing one high-altitude event rather than multiple local crashes. This pattern makes fireballs one of the clearest examples of how spectacular but natural phenomena can produce convincing UFO reports before additional evidence becomes available.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[ised-isde.canada.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Management of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in Canada</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Did That Fireball Really Land Nearby?

One of the strongest recurring misconceptions is that a brilliant meteor appears to disappear behind the nearest ridge or treeline. Many witnesses become convinced it landed only a few kilometres away.

This impression is understandable but misleading. A fireball may first become visible around 80 to 120 kilometres above the ground and remain luminous until roughly 20 to 40 kilometres altitude, depending on its size and composition. Because observers have no reference objects at similar heights, the brain tends to interpret the glowing trail as if it belonged to a much lower object moving through familiar landscape features.

That illusion is especially persuasive in British Columbia. Mountain ridges, coastal inlets and heavily forested valleys create strong foreground silhouettes. A meteor that actually burns up far beyond those ridges can appear to plunge directly into them. Residents on opposite sides of the province may each believe they saw the object land "just over the next hill", even though both are watching the same atmospheric event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalnews.ca/news/3718712/fireball-meteor-british-columbia-alberta-light-sky/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalnews.ca">[Global News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalnews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on globalnews.ca.</span></span></span>

Large regional reporting patterns often reveal the mistake. If people in Victoria, the Fraser Valley, the Okanagan and neighbouring provinces all report an apparently local impact within the same minute, the object cannot have landed near every witness. Instead, investigators can reconstruct a high-altitude trajectory using many independent observations.

## Why Meteors Look Lower and Closer Than They Are

Several features of human perception combine to make fireballs appear deceptively close.


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **No reliable distance cues.** At night there are few visual references against which to judge altitude.
* **Foreground mountains.** A meteor disappearing behind a ridge appears to strike it, even though it may actually be hundreds of kilometres beyond.
* **Extreme brightness.** Exceptionally bright fireballs illuminate the landscape, making them feel physically close despite their great altitude.
* **Rapid motion.** Fast movement across a large section of sky creates the impression of an object descending towards the observer.

</div>

The result is a surprisingly consistent pattern. Emergency services and police sometimes receive calls reporting an aircraft accident or explosion, only to discover that no crash occurred. Instead, astronomers and meteor observers receive matching reports from a wide geographical area, confirming a single atmospheric event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalnews.ca/news/3718712/fireball-meteor-british-columbia-alberta-light-sky/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalnews.ca">[Global News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalnews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on globalnews.ca.</span></span></span>

## How Colour, Trails and Fragmentation Shape Reports

The appearance of a fireball often makes it seem unlike an ordinary meteor.

### Bright colours

Witnesses in British Columbia have described fireballs as vivid green, blue, orange or white. These colours are produced by different combinations of the meteoroid's composition, atmospheric interaction and temperature rather than indicating an unusual craft. Green fireballs, in particular, are memorable and frequently appear in UFO reports simply because they differ from familiar aircraft lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalnews.ca/news/3718712/fireball-meteor-british-columbia-alberta-light-sky/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalnews.ca">[Global News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalnews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on globalnews.ca.</span></span></span>

### Breaking apart

Large meteoroids frequently fragment as increasing atmospheric pressure causes them to break apart. To observers, this can resemble an aircraft disintegrating or several glowing objects separating under intelligent control.

Instead, fragmentation is expected behaviour during atmospheric entry. Multiple glowing pieces often continue along nearly identical paths before fading out.

### Persistent glowing trails

Some bright fireballs leave luminous trails that remain visible for several seconds or even minutes as upper-atmospheric winds distort them. Witnesses sometimes interpret these twisting shapes as hovering objects or smoke from a crash site, although they are simply illuminated gases left behind in the meteor's wake.

## Why Reports from Distant Communities Matter

One of the most valuable clues in evaluating these events is not a single eyewitness account but many independent reports.

When observers from Victoria, Vancouver, the Fraser Valley, Kamloops or the Interior all describe a brilliant fireball within the same minute, investigators gain something impossible from one report alone: triangulation. Differences in viewing angle allow astronomers to estimate the object's altitude, direction and approximate flight path.

Modern investigations increasingly combine:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* eyewitness reports,
* dashboard and security camera recordings,
* all-sky meteor cameras,
* satellite observations,
* and, for the brightest events, infrasound or sonic boom measurements.

</div>

The wider the geographical spread of reports, the less likely the object was a nearby crash and the more likely it was a high-altitude meteor visible across much of western Canada. NASA and the American Meteor Society routinely reconstruct fireball trajectories using hundreds of reports spanning British Columbia, Washington and Oregon, illustrating how one spectacular event can appear local to thousands of separate observers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov/skyfalls/events/20260429-071216" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov">[fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Event 20260429-071216April 29, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 29, 2026</span></span></span>

## British Columbia Examples

Several well-publicised events demonstrate this recurring pattern.

A bright fireball over western Canada in September 2017 prompted numerous calls to police from British Columbia residents who believed they had witnessed an explosion or aircraft crash. Reports arrived from communities spread across southern British Columbia and Alberta before investigators identified the object as a meteor.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalnews.ca/news/3718712/fireball-meteor-british-columbia-alberta-light-sky/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalnews.ca">[Global News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalnews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on globalnews.ca.</span></span></span>

More recently, exceptionally bright meteors visible across the Lower Mainland and Vancouver Island generated hundreds of eyewitness reports. In some cases, witnesses also heard delayed sonic booms that shook buildings, reinforcing the impression that something had exploded nearby. Astronomers instead identified these events as bolide meteors—particularly bright meteors capable of producing shock waves after travelling through the upper atmosphere. ## Why These Events Matter in British Columbia's UFO History

Within the broader history of British Columbia UFO reports, bright fireballs are a reminder that dramatic observations are not necessarily mysterious once multiple sources of evidence are combined.

They also illustrate an important investigative principle. A single witness may sincerely report an object crashing nearby, yet dozens or hundreds of equally sincere witnesses can collectively demonstrate that the object never approached the ground at all. Regional reporting transforms what appears to be a local mystery into a well-understood astronomical event.

The same approach underlies modern Canadian thinking on unexplained aerial observations. Rather than treating dramatic eyewitness testimony as sufficient on its own, investigators increasingly compare reports with astronomical, atmospheric and other observational data before deciding whether an event remains unexplained.     var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
