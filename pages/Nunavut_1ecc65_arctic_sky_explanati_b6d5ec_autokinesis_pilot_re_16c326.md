## Introduction

Autokinesis is one of the best-understood visual illusions in aviation and provides an important framework for interpreting some pilot UFO reports in [Nunavut]({{ 'what-really-lies-behind-nunavuts-ufo/' | relative_url }}). The effect occurs when a person stares at a single point of light against a dark, featureless background. Although the light is stationary, it can appear to drift, accelerate, stop or change direction. In the Canadian Arctic, where flights often take place during prolonged winter darkness over snow, sea ice and sparsely lit terrain, the conditions that encourage this illusion are unusually common. Transport Canada specifically warns pilots about autokinesis as part of normal flight training because it has contributed to aviation accidents and misidentification of lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/handbook-civil-aviation-medical-examiners-tp-13312" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tc.canada.ca">[Transport Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tc.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transport Canada Handbook for Civil Aviation Medical Examiners</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Transport CanadaHandbook for Civil Aviation Medical Examiners - TP 13312May 6, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 6, 2019</span></span></span>

Understanding autokinesis does not mean dismissing [pilot testimony]({{ 'pilot-testimony/' | relative_url }}). Instead, it explains why sincere, trained observers can accurately report what they perceived while still misjudging whether a distant light was actually moving.

## Why do fixed lights seem to move over Nunavut?

Autokinesis is not caused by movement in the light itself. Instead, it results from tiny involuntary eye movements combined with the brain's attempt to interpret a point of light that lacks any surrounding visual reference. Under normal daytime conditions, buildings, trees, mountains or the horizon provide a stable frame against which motion can be judged. During an Arctic night flight, many of those references disappear.

A pilot looking at a bright star, planet or distant ground light for several seconds may therefore experience the impression that it is:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-stack" markdown="1">

* drifting sideways;
* climbing or descending;
* pacing the aircraft;
* approaching before suddenly stopping; or
* making abrupt changes of direction.

</div>

Transport Canada's aviation medical guidance specifically notes that stars and distant ground lights watched intently at night can appear to move and be mistaken for other aircraft. The guidance emphasises that this is a recognised physiological limitation rather than a failure of pilot competence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/handbook-civil-aviation-medical-examiners-tp-13312" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tc.canada.ca">[Transport Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tc.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transport Canada Handbook for Civil Aviation Medical Examiners</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Transport CanadaHandbook for Civil Aviation Medical Examiners - TP 13312May 6, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 6, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why Arctic flights are especially vulnerable

Nunavut combines several factors that make autokinesis more persuasive than in many southern flying environments.

First, long periods of darkness mean pilots may observe the same unidentified light for many minutes rather than only briefly. Extended viewing increases the opportunity for the illusion to develop.

Second, much of the territory offers remarkably few visual reference points. Flights between communities, mines and remote airstrips frequently cross hundreds of kilometres of frozen coastline, sea ice or tundra with almost no artificial lighting. Without nearby landmarks, estimating a light's distance or true motion becomes far more difficult.

Third, crews often make small heading, altitude or bank corrections during routine flight. Because the aircraft itself is moving, an apparently fixed celestial object may seem to respond to those manoeuvres. Human perception naturally attributes the changing geometry to the light rather than to the observer's own motion.

Finally, bright stars and planets often appear exceptionally prominent under the Arctic's dark, clear winter skies. A very bright celestial object can become the focus of prolonged observation, creating ideal conditions for autokinesis even before atmospheric effects such as twinkling or colour changes are considered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/transport-canada-aeronautical-information-manual-tc-aim-tp-14371" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tc.canada.ca">[Transport Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tc.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Transport CanadaTransport Canada Aeronautical Information Manual (TC AIM) - TP 14371...</span></span></span>

## The 2018 northern Baffin pilot report

One of Nunavut's best-known modern aviation UFO reports illustrates why autokinesis is considered alongside more conventional astronomical explanations.

On 24 November 2018, pilots flying a Nolinor Aviation Boeing 737 from Iqaluit towards the Mary River mine reported an unusual bright light over northern Baffin Island. The occurrence was entered into Transport Canada's Civil Aviation Daily Occurrence Reporting System (CADORS), and the report was passed to NORAD through established aviation reporting procedures. Importantly, the report stated that flight operations were unaffected.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nunatsiaq.com/stories/article/65674pilots_spot_possible_ufo_above_nunavuts_northern_baffin_island/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nunatsiaq.com">[Nunatsiaq News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nunatsiaq.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">News Pilots report UFO sighting over Nunavut’s northern Baffin Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nunatsiaq NewsPilots report UFO sighting over Nunavut’s northern Baffin IslandNovember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 27, 2018</span></span></span>

According to later comments reported by Nunatsiaq News, the observed light appeared to change between red, green and white. The airline's vice-president explained that after speaking with the pilot, the crew themselves considered a natural explanation—possibly a star or meteor—to remain likely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nunatsiaq.com/stories/article/65674pilots_spot_possible_ufo_above_nunavuts_northern_baffin_island/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nunatsiaq.com">[Nunatsiaq News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nunatsiaq.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">News Pilots report UFO sighting over Nunavut’s northern Baffin Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nunatsiaq NewsPilots report UFO sighting over Nunavut’s northern Baffin IslandNovember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 27, 2018</span></span></span>

That combination of observations is significant because colour-changing stationary lights are consistent with atmospheric [scintillation]({{ 'scintillation/' | relative_url }}), while apparent movement during sustained viewing is compatible with autokinesis. Neither explanation can be proven after the event because there were no publicly available photographs, radar tracks or precise astronomical reconstruction tied to the crew's exact line of sight. Nevertheless, the known viewing conditions fit recognised aviation illusions without requiring extraordinary assumptions.

## Why pilot observations still deserve careful attention

Autokinesis should not be used to dismiss every pilot UFO report.

Pilots receive extensive training in visual scanning, aircraft recognition and night operations. They also understand the operational importance of accurately reporting unusual hazards. The fact that trained crews can experience autokinesis is precisely why Transport Canada and aviation medicine include the illusion in standard safety guidance. Experience reduces the risk of misinterpretation but cannot eliminate the underlying physiology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/transport-canada-aeronautical-information-manual-tc-aim-tp-14371" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tc.canada.ca">[Transport Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tc.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Transport CanadaTransport Canada Aeronautical Information Manual (TC AIM) - TP 14371...</span></span></span>

Equally important, a report of an unidentified light does not automatically imply that the crew believed they had encountered an extraordinary craft. Aviation reporting systems encourage pilots to document unusual observations even when the cause is uncertain. ## The limits of pilot observations

Autokinesis highlights an important distinction between an honest observation and a confirmed identification.

A pilot may reliably report:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* seeing a bright light;
* its apparent colour;
* the duration of the sighting;
* where it appeared relative to the aircraft; and
* how it seemed to behave.

</div>

Those observations are valuable evidence. What becomes much less reliable under Arctic night conditions is the inferred behaviour of the light itself. Without independent references, observers cannot confidently determine whether a light actually changed course, accelerated, maintained formation or remained stationary.

For investigators examining Nunavut UFO reports, this means that isolated visual sightings carry less evidential weight than cases supported by multiple independent observation points, calibrated imaging, radar data or other corroborating measurements. When only a single distant light is involved, autokinesis remains one of the most plausible mechanisms that can transform an ordinary astronomical or terrestrial light into a convincing report of apparent aerial manoeuvres.

    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
