## Introduction

A surprisingly thick government file does not necessarily mean that several people witnessed the same unusual event. In [Alberta]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-albertas-ufo/' | relative_url }}), as elsewhere in Canada, a single UFO report could be copied, forwarded and re-filed by several federal and provincial bodies, creating multiple archival records from one original account. That administrative process is one of the easiest ways to overestimate the apparent weight of historical UFO evidence.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Alberta_1dc61b_alberta_official_ufo_8ad502_alberta_ufo_duplicat_16ef80-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Duplicate Files illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Alberta_1dc61b_alberta_official_ufo_8ad502_alberta_ufo_duplicat_16ef80-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Alberta_1dc61b_alberta_official_ufo_8ad502_alberta_ufo_duplicat_16ef80-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

Understanding how records moved through the Canadian system helps explain why an Alberta sighting might appear repeatedly in government archives without representing independent confirmation. The paper trail mainly reflects how officials shared information about aviation safety, defence, policing and scientific assessment—not how many witnesses existed or how convincing they found the report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

## How reports moved between Canadian agencies

Canada did not operate a single national UFO office for most of the Cold War. Instead, reports followed the responsibilities of whichever organisation first received them.

A typical Alberta case could pass through several stages:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. **A witness reports an unusual object** to a local RCMP detachment, airport, military unit or Transport Canada office.
2. **The receiving office prepares its own paperwork**, recording the witness statement and any immediate observations.
3. **Copies are forwarded** to agencies with relevant responsibilities, such as the Department of National Defence for possible air-defence concerns or the National Research Council (NRC) for scientific assessment.
4. **Each organisation files the correspondence independently**, often assigning its own file number, cover sheet or internal memorandum.
5. **Replies and follow-up letters** generate further documents, even if no new evidence has emerged.

</div>

As a result, one observation could produce witness statements, covering letters, routing slips, internal memoranda, scientific comments and filing records spread across several departmental collections. When those collections were later transferred to Library and Archives Canada, each record remained part of its original departmental archive rather than being merged into a single master file.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[canada.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

## When copied records look like corroboration

This administrative history creates one of the most common traps for researchers.

Suppose an Alberta incident appears in:

* an RCMP report, * a Department of National Defence memorandum,<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ca-foia-part-29-canada-foia-part-29-pages" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[ufotransparency.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ca foia part 29 canada foia part 29 pages</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">intl ca foia part 29 canada foia part 29 pages</span></span></span> * a Department of Transport file,<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ca-foia-part-29-canada-foia-part-29-pages" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[ufotransparency.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ca foia part 29 canada foia part 29 pages</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">intl ca foia part 29 canada foia part 29 pages</span></span></span>
* and an NRC assessment.

At first glance, that can resemble four separate investigations or four independent confirmations.

In reality, those documents may all derive from exactly the same witness account. The later files often quote, summarise or attach the earlier report rather than introducing new observations. Additional paperwork may simply record that one department acknowledged receipt, requested advice or closed the file after determining that no further action was required.

This distinction is important because government involvement demonstrates that officials considered the report worth recording, not that they verified the witness's interpretation. The administrative effort reflects Canadian reporting procedures as much as the underlying event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why Alberta reports often entered several systems

Alberta's geography made overlapping responsibilities particularly likely.

The province contains major civilian airports, military facilities, extensive commercial aviation routes and large remote areas where local police or pilots might be the first to report an unusual aerial observation. Depending on where an incident occurred, several legitimate questions could arise simultaneously:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Could it affect aircraft safety?
* Could it represent a defence concern?
* Was it simply an astronomical or atmospheric phenomenon?
* Did police need to document a public complaint?

</div>

Different agencies answered different questions. Their parallel interests naturally produced parallel paperwork.

During the Cold War especially, officials could not assume that an unidentified object reported near air routes or military infrastructure was harmless without at least considering conventional [explanations]({{ 'explanations/' | relative_url }}) such as aircraft, balloons, meteors or other identifiable phenomena. That practical approach explains why routine administrative exchanges are common within Canadian UFO archives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

## How to trace a report back to its source

Researchers who want to understand an Alberta sighting should work backwards rather than counting files.

Useful questions include:

* **Which document contains the original witness statement?**
* **Which documents merely quote or forward that statement?**
* **Did later files add genuinely new observations or only administrative correspondence?**
* **Were there independent witnesses, or just repeated references to the same witness?**
* **Did different agencies reach separate conclusions based on new evidence, or simply acknowledge receipt?**

Library and Archives Canada itself advises caution when searching the collection. The catalogue reproduces information as it appeared on the original records, many documents lack complete location details, and searches by place or date may produce only partial results. A large number of retrieved documents therefore should not be treated as a count of independent sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

## What multiple files actually prove

Multiple government files are valuable because they document how Canadian institutions handled unusual aerial reports. They can reveal reporting procedures, departmental responsibilities, timelines and official reasoning.

They do **not**, by themselves, demonstrate that multiple witnesses saw the same object, that several agencies independently verified an unexplained craft, or that officials concluded an extraterrestrial explanation was likely.

For Alberta's historical UFO record, the safest interpretation is straightforward: a substantial paper trail usually shows that information travelled through Canada's administrative system. Before treating several archival files as multiple pieces of evidence, it is essential to establish whether they all originate from one original report or from genuinely independent observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[canada.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.caMarch 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
