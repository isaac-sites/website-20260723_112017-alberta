## Introduction

The phrase **“non-meteoritic”** is one of the most misunderstood labels in Canada's historical UFO records. Readers often assume it meant that officials had ruled out every ordinary explanation and were left with evidence of an extraordinary craft. That is not what the label meant.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Ontario_f9f742_ontario_ufo_archives_181740_non_meteoritic_files_832d56-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Non Meteoritic Files illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Ontario_f9f742_ontario_ufo_archives_181740_non_meteoritic_files_832d56-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Ontario_f9f742_ontario_ufo_archives_181740_non_meteoritic_files_832d56-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

For [Ontario]({{ 'what-does-ontarios-ufo-record-really/' | relative_url }}) reports held in the National Research Council of Canada (NRC) files, **“non-meteoritic” was primarily an administrative classification**. It indicated that a reported aerial event could not be confidently filed as a meteor or other conventional meteor observation within the NRC's astronomical work. It did **not** mean investigators had concluded that an alien spacecraft, secret aircraft or other exotic object had been identified. Many reports remained in the file simply because the available information was incomplete, ambiguous or unsuitable for a firm conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

Understanding that distinction is essential when reading Ontario's [UFO archives]({{ 'ufo-archives-1f7b5b/' | relative_url }}). The files demonstrate how the Canadian government organised uncertain reports—not that it verified extraordinary explanations.

## How the National Research Council used the label

The National Research Council became the principal federal recipient for many civilian UFO reports from the late 1960s until the mid-1990s. This responsibility grew partly because the Council already possessed expertise in astronomy and meteor observation, making it a natural destination for reports of unusual lights in the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

Astronomer **Peter Millman**, well known for his work on meteors, reviewed many of these reports. His first question was typically whether an observation could be explained through known astronomical phenomena. If it clearly matched a meteor, it belonged with meteor records. If it did not, the report might instead be filed as **non-meteoritic**.

That filing decision reflected the limits of classification rather than a positive identification. The label simply separated reports that were **not convincingly meteor-related** from those that were.

In practical terms, a report might be placed in the non-meteoritic file because:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the reported duration was much longer than a typical meteor;
* witnesses described hovering or repeated manoeuvres inconsistent with a meteor;
* the observation occurred under circumstances where a meteor explanation appeared unlikely;
* the description was too vague or contradictory to support any confident astronomical classification;
* investigators lacked sufficient information to reach any firm conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Ordinary explanations officials still considered

Being placed in the non-meteoritic file did **not** end the search for ordinary explanations. Officials continued to consider numerous possibilities before leaving a report unresolved.

Depending on the circumstances, they examined whether the sighting could involve:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* conventional aircraft;
* military flights;
* bright planets or stars viewed under unusual atmospheric conditions;
* satellites;
* balloons;
* atmospheric optical effects;
* incomplete or mistaken witness observations.

</div>

The important point is that **"not a meteor" is only one eliminated possibility**. It does not automatically elevate every remaining explanation to something mysterious.

This distinction is especially important for Ontario because reports came from many different settings, including major urban centres, rural communities, [Great Lakes]({{ 'great-lakes/' | relative_url }}) locations and aviation routes. A light seen over Toronto, Ottawa or northern Ontario might require very different explanations depending on weather, viewing angle, nearby airports, military activity or local geography. The non-meteoritic label itself carried none of that interpretive weight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why unresolved reports remained in the file

Modern readers sometimes assume that an unresolved report represents strong evidence for an unknown craft. The historical record suggests something more cautious.

Many reports remained unresolved because investigators lacked the information needed to make any reliable determination. Typical limitations included:

* uncertain times or dates;
* approximate locations;
* no photographs or physical evidence;
* only a single witness;
* conflicting descriptions;
* no radar or [aviation records]({{ 'aviation-records/' | relative_url }}) to compare against;
* correspondence ending before further enquiries could be completed.

Rather than invent an explanation, officials often left these reports without a definitive conclusion. In archival practice, **"unresolved" frequently means "insufficient evidence," not "confirmed anomaly."**<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

This conservative approach reflects standard scientific and administrative practice. When evidence cannot support a confident identification, uncertainty is recorded instead of replaced with speculation.

## Ontario examples show the filing system, not proof

Library and Archives Canada preserves numerous Ontario reports within National Research Council records, including files explicitly described as reports on **non-meteoric sightings**. One example is a 1965 Cochrane, Ontario record catalogued within the NRC series. The existence of such files demonstrates that unusual aerial observations were formally documented and preserved. It does **not** demonstrate that investigators concluded those observations involved extraordinary vehicles.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/databases/ufo/001057-119.01-e.php?PHPSESSID=taq6tkh1mabsust3353mthnd63&amp;interval=&amp;isn_id_nbr=1017&amp;record_id=1017" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: collectionscanada.gc.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">collectionscanada.gc.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada ARCHIVED</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaARCHIVED - Item Display - Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The Search for the Unknown - Library and Archives CanadaJuly 30, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 30, 1965</span></span></span>

Across the wider Ontario collection, the archive contains correspondence, witness statements, memoranda and [reporting forms]({{ 'reporting-form/' | relative_url }}) rather than a single investigative programme with uniform standards. Some reports received substantial attention, while others consist only of brief descriptions or exchanges between departments deciding whether further action was appropriate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/science-technology/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">s UFOs: The search for the unknown</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canada&#x27;s UFOs: The search for the unknown - Canada.ca...</span></span></span>

The historical value of these records therefore lies in documenting what was reported and how Canadian institutions handled uncertainty, rather than providing definitive answers about individual sightings.

## Why the misunderstanding persists

The phrase **"non-meteoritic"** sounds more dramatic today than it did within the NRC's filing system.

Modern readers often encounter the label without its administrative context and naturally interpret it as a judgement that something extraordinary had occurred. Popular discussions sometimes reinforce this misunderstanding by treating the category as though it meant "unexplainable."

The documentary evidence points in the opposite direction. The label was designed to distinguish one class of reports from meteor observations, not to certify unknown craft. Government reviews of Canada's historical UFO reporting have explicitly described the files as records accumulated for multiple purposes—including explaining observations, investigating some reports and responding to public enquiries—rather than as evidence that unexplained cases were confirmed mysteries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## What the non-meteoritic files really tell us

Ontario's non-meteoritic UFO files are valuable precisely because they preserve uncertainty honestly.

They show that Canadian officials:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* recorded unusual aerial reports rather than automatically discarding them;
* distinguished meteor observations from other kinds of sightings;
* continued considering conventional explanations even after ruling out a meteor;
* accepted that some reports could not be confidently identified with the evidence available.

</div>

That makes the files historically important but evidentially modest. They demonstrate that unexplained observations existed in the official record and that government agencies maintained procedures for handling them. They do **not** demonstrate that officials concluded extraordinary craft were operating over Ontario. Instead, they illustrate a careful administrative approach in which "non-meteoritic" meant exactly what it said: **not confidently identified as a meteor—and nothing more.**<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/office-chief-science-advisor/sky-canada-project/management-public-reporting-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-canada" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ised-isde.canada.ca">[Innovation Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ised-isde.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Innovation CanadaManagement of Public Reporting of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in CanadaJune 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2025</span></span></span>

    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
