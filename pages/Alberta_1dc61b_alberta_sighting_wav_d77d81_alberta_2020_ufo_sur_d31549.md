## Introduction

[Alberta]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-albertas-ufo/' | relative_url }}) experienced an unusually large increase in reported UFO sightings during 2020, with roughly 150 reports recorded before the provincial total fell back to about 75 in 2021. The spike is best understood as a surge in **reporting**, not as evidence that unexplained objects suddenly became more common over the province. Researchers, astronomers and journalists have instead pointed to a combination of pandemic-related lifestyle changes, increased public attention to the night sky, the highly visible deployment of SpaceX's Starlink satellites, and greater willingness to report unusual observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2022/09/16/alberta-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">City News Calgary Alberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgaryAlberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author - CityNews CalgarySeptember 16, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 16, 2022</span></span></span>

Within Alberta's wider history of [sighting waves]({{ 'sighting-waves/' | relative_url }}), 2020 stands out because several independent factors overlapped at the same time. That makes it an important case study in how social circumstances can influence UFO statistics without necessarily changing the number of genuinely unexplained aerial phenomena.

## How Alberta's report total changed

The increase in Alberta mirrored a broader national pattern. The Canadian UFO Survey recorded 1,243 reports across Canada during 2020, a rise of around 46 per cent compared with 2019 and one of the highest totals since the survey began. Alberta contributed a noticeably larger share of reports than in the following year, before numbers returned to more typical levels during 2021.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2021/03/30/survey-suggests-canadians-looked-to-the-skies-saw-more-ufos-during-pandemic/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[citynews.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgarySurvey suggests Canadians looked to the skies, saw more UFOs during pandemicMarch 30, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2021</span></span></span>

The sharp decline from approximately 150 Alberta reports in 2020 to about 75 in 2021 is significant because it argues against a permanent change in whatever people were observing. If an entirely new class of unexplained object had become common over Alberta, researchers would expect elevated reporting to continue. Instead, the rapid fall suggests that temporary conditions affecting observation and reporting were more influential.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2022/09/16/alberta-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">City News Calgary Alberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgaryAlberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author - CityNews CalgarySeptember 16, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 16, 2022</span></span></span>

Chris Rutkowski, who has coordinated the long-running Canadian UFO Survey, has cautioned against attributing the increase to a single cause. He has noted that the pandemic clearly had "an effect of some sort", but that the precise reasons remain uncertain because several influences operated simultaneously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2022/09/16/alberta-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">City News Calgary Alberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgaryAlberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author - CityNews CalgarySeptember 16, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 16, 2022</span></span></span>

## Pandemic routines, publicity and Starlink

No single explanation accounts for Alberta's 2020 surge. Instead, several well-supported factors reinforced one another.

**More people were looking at the sky.** During COVID-19 restrictions many Albertans spent more evenings at home, outdoors or in their gardens. Travel patterns changed, organised entertainment declined and amateur astronomy experienced renewed interest across Canada. Astronomers reported increased demand for telescopes while also observing that many newcomers were seeing familiar celestial objects for the first time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2021/05/02/aliens-are-coming-alberta-rcmp-911-dispatchers-fielding-calls-about-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on citynews.ca.</span></span></span>

**People were more likely to report what they saw.** Increased media attention to unidentified aerial phenomena, online discussion groups and simplified online reporting channels meant observations that might once have remained private were more often submitted to investigators. A reporting increase does not necessarily indicate an increase in unusual events; it can simply mean more witnesses decide to file reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2021/03/30/survey-suggests-canadians-looked-to-the-skies-saw-more-ufos-during-pandemic/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgarySurvey suggests Canadians looked to the skies, saw more UFOs during pandemicMarch 30, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2021</span></span></span>

**Starlink created a genuinely new visual phenomenon.** During 2020, SpaceX launched large numbers of Starlink satellites. Shortly after launch, these satellites often appeared as striking "trains" of bright lights moving silently across the evening sky. For observers unfamiliar with satellite constellations, the formations looked unlike traditional aircraft or single satellites and generated numerous UFO reports in Canada and elsewhere. Astronomical studies also confirmed that early Starlink satellites were bright enough to be easily visible to the naked eye under suitable conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2021/05/02/aliens-are-coming-alberta-rcmp-911-dispatchers-fielding-calls-about-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[citynews.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on citynews.ca.</span></span></span>

**Highly publicised sky events encouraged further observation.** Comet NEOWISE became widely visible during the summer of 2020, drawing many people outdoors specifically to observe the night sky. Increased sky-watching naturally increased opportunities to notice satellites, planets, aircraft and other unfamiliar lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://globalnews.ca/news/7223495/canadian-ufo-sightings-down-2019/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalnews.ca">[Global News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalnews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">canadian ufo sightings down 2019</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Global NewsCanadian UFO sightings down in 2019, but expected to rise amid coronavirus pandemic &#124; Globalnews.ca...</span></span></span>

These influences worked together rather than independently. Someone who rarely looked at the night sky before 2020 might have gone outside to see Comet NEOWISE, noticed a newly launched Starlink satellite train, discussed it on social media and then submitted a UFO report—all without any genuinely unknown object being involved.

## Why the surge cannot prove more unknown craft

The 2020 increase is often misunderstood because higher report numbers can appear dramatic. However, UFO databases measure **reported observations**, not confirmed unidentified objects.

Most Canadian UFO reports eventually receive ordinary [explanations]({{ 'explanations/' | relative_url }}) such as:

* aircraft viewed from unusual angles;
* bright planets or stars;
* meteors and [fireballs]({{ 'fireballs/' | relative_url }});
* satellites, including Starlink;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/explainers/starlink-satellites-ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 16, 2026 — Skywatching / Updated 2026-07-16 / 8 min read STARLINK SATELLITES MISTAKEN FOR UFOS: WHY SATELLITE TRAINS LOOK SO STRANGE...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 16, 2026</span></span></span>
* balloons or atmospheric effects.

## What 2020 tells us about Alberta's sighting waves

The 2020 reporting surge illustrates an important principle that applies throughout Alberta's UFO history: sighting waves often reflect changes in **human observation** as much as changes in the sky itself.

Alberta's wide horizons and dark rural skies make aerial objects especially noticeable, but whether those observations become official UFO reports depends on social conditions. A period of heightened public curiosity, intensive media coverage or the appearance of a new class of highly visible satellite can temporarily double reporting without requiring any increase in unexplained phenomena.

For that reason, historians and investigators generally treat 2020 as a reporting wave rather than evidence that Alberta experienced an exceptional concentration of unknown craft. The year remains significant because it demonstrates how technological change, public attention and everyday behaviour can reshape UFO statistics—and why report totals alone cannot answer the question of what people were actually seeing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://calgary.citynews.ca/2022/09/16/alberta-ufo-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: calgary.citynews.ca">[CityNews Calgary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">calgary.citynews.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">City News Calgary Alberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CityNews CalgaryAlberta &#x27;holds its own&#x27; in UFO sightings: Author - CityNews CalgarySeptember 16, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 16, 2022</span></span></span>

    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
