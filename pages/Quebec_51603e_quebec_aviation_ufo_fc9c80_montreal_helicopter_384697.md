## Introduction

A widely shared video recorded over Montreal in early March 2023 appeared, at first glance, to show a single large object carrying an unusual pattern of bright lights across the night sky. The footage quickly circulated on social media as a possible UFO sighting and was viewed hundreds of thousands of times. Within days, however, the most credible explanation emerged: the lights belonged to four Royal Canadian Air Force CH-146 Griffon helicopters flying in close formation rather than to one unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newsweek.com/ufo-montreal-video-twitter-identified-1787925" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsweek.com">[Newsweek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times - NewsweekMarch 15, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 15, 2023</span></span></span>

The incident has become a useful modern example within [Quebec]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-quebecs-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO history because it demonstrates how multiple conventional aircraft can be perceived as one structured object when darkness conceals their airframes. Rather than diminishing the value of eyewitness testimony, the case shows why reports involving unusual lights over busy [aviation]({{ 'aviation/' | relative_url }}) regions need to be reconstructed using flight activity, viewing geometry and later identification before drawing extraordinary conclusions.

## How the clustered lights appeared to witnesses

On the evening of 2 March 2023, numerous residents across the Montreal area noticed an unusual formation of lights moving at relatively low altitude. Videos posted online showed four bright lights arranged in what appeared to be a rigid geometric pattern. Because the helicopters themselves were almost invisible against the night sky, many viewers perceived the lights as belonging to a single large object rather than several separate aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.tvanouvelles.ca/2023/03/03/en-images--un-escadron-dhelicopteres-fait-tourner-les-tetes-a-montreal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tvanouvelles.ca">[TVA Nouvelles]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tvanouvelles.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TVA NouvellesEN IMAGES &#124; Un mystérieux escadron d’hélicoptères fait tourner les têtes à Montréal &#124; TVA NouvellesMarch 3, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 3, 2023</span></span></span>

One widely circulated recording captured two separate fly-pasts roughly twelve minutes apart. The person filming repeatedly stated that the object did not resemble an ordinary aeroplane and commented that the first pass produced a deep humming sound while the second sounded more like multiple aircraft. Those reactions helped the footage spread rapidly through UFO-focused social media accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newsweek.com/ufo-montreal-video-twitter-identified-1787925" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsweek.com">[Newsweek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times - NewsweekMarch 15, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 15, 2023</span></span></span>

The witness's confidence that they were "not near an airport" also illustrates a common feature of aviation-related misidentifications. Military helicopters do not need to follow the same visual expectations as commercial airliners, and Montreal's wider airspace includes military, civilian and transit routes extending well beyond the immediate vicinity of Montréal–Trudeau Airport.

## Why darkness merged several helicopters into one shape

The visual illusion depended less on unusual aircraft than on the way human perception interprets distant lights at night.

Several factors combined:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **The helicopters' fuselages were difficult to see.** Only navigation and landing lights were readily visible from the ground.
* **The aircraft maintained a disciplined formation.** Their relative positions changed very little from the observer's viewpoint, encouraging the impression of a rigid object.
* **Night conditions removed depth cues.** Without visible airframes or reference points, observers could not judge the separation between individual helicopters.
* **Bright lights dominated the scene.** Human vision naturally groups nearby lights into coherent shapes, particularly when the structures producing those lights are hidden.

</div>

This is a well-known limitation of night-time observation rather than evidence of deception or unreliable witnesses. Even experienced observers can underestimate the spacing between aircraft when only their lights remain visible. In this case, four helicopters separated by tens or hundreds of metres could visually compress into what appeared to be one large triangular or diamond-shaped craft.

The witness's description of a low-frequency humming also fits a conventional explanation. Multiple helicopter rotor systems operating together can produce complex overlapping sound patterns, especially as echoes reflect from buildings in an urban environment. The resulting noise may seem different from that produced by a single helicopter passing overhead.

## How the later identification changed the case

As the footage gained attention, independent investigators compared the timing and appearance of the lights with known military aviation activity. The explanation that attracted the strongest supporting evidence identified the aircraft as four Royal Canadian Air Force CH-146 Griffon helicopters flying together. That assessment was subsequently reinforced by reporting from Canadian news organisations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newsweek.com/ufo-montreal-video-twitter-identified-1787925" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsweek.com">[Newsweek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Identified After Video Viewed Nearly Half a Million Times - NewsweekMarch 15, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 15, 2023</span></span></span>

Local reporting went beyond social media speculation by obtaining confirmation from Canada's Department of National Defence. According to departmental spokespeople, the aircraft were four CH-146 Griffons from 430 Tactical Helicopter Squadron returning to Canadian Forces Base Valcartier after a training exercise in the United States. They passed through the Montreal area before continuing towards their home base.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.tvanouvelles.ca/2023/03/03/en-images--un-escadron-dhelicopteres-fait-tourner-les-tetes-a-montreal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tvanouvelles.ca">[TVA Nouvelles]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tvanouvelles.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TVA NouvellesEN IMAGES &#124; Un mystérieux escadron d’hélicoptères fait tourner les têtes à Montréal &#124; TVA NouvellesMarch 3, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 3, 2023</span></span></span>

Some early media reports speculated that the helicopters might instead belong to 438 Tactical Helicopter Squadron at Saint-Hubert, reflecting the fact that Griffons are also based in the Montreal region. Later reporting citing the Department of National Defence clarified that the aircraft involved were from 430 Squadron and were simply transiting the city after training.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.24heures.ca/2023/03/03/un-mysterieux-groupe-dhelicopteres-passe-au-dessus-de-montreal-et-cree-la-confusion" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: 24heures.ca">[24heures.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">24heures.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 3, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 3, 2023</span></span></span>

Once that information became available, the case shifted from an unexplained sighting to a documented example of conventional military aircraft producing an unexpectedly striking visual effect.

## What the incident contributes to Quebec's UFO history

The Montreal helicopter formation is valuable precisely because it is well documented and ultimately identifiable.

Unlike many historical UFO reports that remain unresolved due to missing evidence, this case includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* numerous independent witnesses;
* multiple videos recorded from different locations;
* a specific aircraft type;
* confirmation from the Department of National Defence reported by established Canadian media; and

    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
