## Introduction

The available evidence does **not** show that the bright object reported near Ebenezer, [Prince Edward Island]({{ 'prince-edward-island/' | relative_url }}), in August 1990 actually landed in the nearby woods. Witnesses consistently described what appeared to be a luminous object descending behind the treeline, but no [confirmed]({{ 'confirmed/' | relative_url }}) impact site, debris field, meteorite or recovered object was ever found. The most plausible explanation is that observers saw a distant fireball approaching the horizon, creating a powerful visual impression that it had come down close by. That does not mean the witnesses were mistaken about seeing a real phenomenon. Rather, it highlights how bright fireballs near the horizon can appear far closer than they really are, making an atmospheric event seem to end just beyond local trees or fields.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skyandtelescope.org/stargazing-and-observing/celestial-objects-to-watch/about-meteors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skyandtelescope.org">[Sky &amp; Telescope]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skyandtelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky &amp; Telescope About Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky &amp; TelescopeAbout Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!November 1, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2012</span></span></span>

## What the witnesses actually saw

The key claim in the [Ebenezer]({{ 'ebenezer/' | relative_url }}) case is not that anyone watched a solid object strike the ground. Instead, witnesses reported a brilliant light that appeared to descend into woodland near their homes before disappearing from direct view.

This distinction matters. Seeing an object vanish behind a tree line is very different from observing a confirmed impact. The surviving accounts describe an apparent descent, followed by reports of a lingering glow visible through the trees. Contemporary searches the following day failed to locate physical evidence of a crash or landing, despite interest from local observers and astronomers.

Within the wider August 1990 incident, reports from across Atlantic Canada are also consistent with a bright regional fireball visible over a large area. Such events can easily give many observers the impression that the object came down nearby, even when they are separated by hundreds of kilometres.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skyandtelescope.org/stargazing-and-observing/celestial-objects-to-watch/about-meteors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skyandtelescope.org">[Sky &amp; Telescope]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skyandtelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky &amp; Telescope About Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky &amp; TelescopeAbout Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!November 1, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2012</span></span></span>

## How horizon effects can mimic a landing

The strongest explanation for the apparent "landing" comes from well-understood features of human perception rather than from evidence of a physical impact.

Bright [fireballs]({{ 'fireballs-e5c642/' | relative_url }}) usually become luminous tens of kilometres above the Earth's surface and often cease glowing while still high in the atmosphere. Because they are exceptionally bright, observers have few reliable visual cues for judging their true distance. A fireball descending towards the horizon therefore seems to drop behind nearby hills or woodland even though it may still be many kilometres away and at considerable altitude.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skyandtelescope.org/stargazing-and-observing/celestial-objects-to-watch/about-meteors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skyandtelescope.org">[skyandtelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skyandtelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky &amp; Telescope About Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky &amp; TelescopeAbout Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!November 1, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2012</span></span></span>

Several factors reinforce this illusion:

* **Loss of depth perception.** Against a dark sky there are few reference points for estimating distance.
* **The horizon effect.** An object moving away from the observer appears to sink below the horizon, giving the impression of entering the ground.
* **Foreground features.** Trees and woodland can obscure the final visible portion of a distant trajectory, making it appear that the object disappeared into the forest.
* **Extreme brightness.** Very bright fireballs overwhelm normal visual cues and often look much closer than they really are.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skyandtelescope.org/stargazing-and-observing/celestial-objects-to-watch/about-meteors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skyandtelescope.org">[Sky &amp; Telescope]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skyandtelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky &amp; Telescope About Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky &amp; TelescopeAbout Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!November 1, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2012</span></span></span>

Astronomers have long recognised this effect. Even experienced observers have occasionally believed that a meteor landed only a short distance away when later reconstruction showed it was still tens of kilometres above the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skyandtelescope.org/stargazing-and-observing/celestial-objects-to-watch/about-meteors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skyandtelescope.org">[Sky &amp; Telescope]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skyandtelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sky &amp; Telescope About Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sky &amp; TelescopeAbout Meteors, Meteor Showers, Fireballs and More!November 1, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2012</span></span></span>

## Why no impact site was confirmed

If a sizeable object had genuinely struck the woods near Ebenezer, investigators would normally expect at least some supporting evidence.

No confirmed impact crater was reported. No meteorite fragments were recovered. No authenticated debris from an aircraft, satellite or unidentified craft emerged. Searches conducted after the event failed to identify a physical landing site despite local interest.

This absence does not prove that nothing unusual was seen. It simply means the strongest physical test of the landing claim produced no supporting evidence.

The lack of a verified impact also makes later stories about recovered objects or military activity difficult to evaluate. Those accounts appeared years afterwards and rely largely on anonymous testimony rather than contemporaneous documentation. Without independently verified physical evidence, they cannot establish that the original light actually reached the ground.

## What remains difficult to explain

The principal reason the Ebenezer incident continues to attract attention is not the reported descent itself but the claim that a glow remained visible in the wooded area for an extended period afterwards.

A typical meteor would not normally produce a stationary luminous object on the ground for hours. That leaves several possibilities, none of which has been confirmed:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the glow was unrelated to the original fireball but happened to appear in the same direction;
* atmospheric or perspective effects altered how a distant light was perceived through the trees;
* witness recollections became more detailed over time as the story was retold;
* an unidentified but ordinary terrestrial light source contributed to the observation.

</div>

No explanation has been demonstrated conclusively because no physical evidence survives to test these alternatives.

## The most balanced assessment

Taken on its own, the claim that the Ebenezer object landed nearby is weakly supported. The witness testimony is sincere and consistent in describing an apparent descent, but apparent descent is not the same as a verified landing.

The broader regional context strengthens the case for a genuine fireball crossing Atlantic Canada while simultaneously weakening the idea of a nearby crash. Fireballs are well known for creating convincing illusions of local impacts, particularly when they disappear low on the horizon.   function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
